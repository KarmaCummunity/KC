# 06 — Domains (superseded by {{D:one-world-list}})

The "domain" layer (D45: one internal tag per post, plus a light domain hub, S37–S39) was removed on 2026-11-01 by the PM's one-world-list decision. There is one list of giving worlds, and what used to be a domain is now either a world of its own or a sub-category of a world. The table below records where each former domain went so older references stay readable; the binding definitions live in [04](./04_product_structure.md) and [05](./05_giving_worlds.md).

| Former domain | slug | Now | Rules carried over |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health & medicine | medical | **World** `medical` ([05 §5.8](./05_giving_worlds.md#58-medical-medical--directory-now-feed-later)) | Only verified medical bodies publish (D30, S17); no medical advice; blood calls only from medical bodies; local shortage → targeted emergency (D42) |
| Animals | animals | **World** `animals` ([05 §5.9](./05_giving_worlds.md#59-animals-animals--phase-2)) | Everyone may publish and take (PM 2026-11-02); free only; SPCA as lead partner |
| Environment | environment | **Removed**: recycling, plants, seeds → items; cleanups → time; cleanup challenges → challenges | Hazardous waste only via a licensed body (kept as an items rule) |
| Elderly & loneliness | elderly | **Sub-category of time** | Home visit requires volunteer ID verification (D48); calls / errands do not |
| Democracy | democracy | **World** `democracy` ([05 §5.10](./05_giving_worlds.md#510-democracy-democracy--phase-3)) | Strict party-political neutrality (S25); governance in [11](./11_governance.md) |
| Philanthropic employment | employment | **World** `employment` ([05 §5.11](./05_giving_worlds.md#511-employment-employment--phase-2)) | Only verified organizations that may legally employ post jobs; free posting; no karma on paid jobs (S27) |
| Sport | sport | **Sub-category of time** (and of items for equipment) | No commercial competitive sport |
| Science & technology | tech | **Sub-category of knowledge** | Devices handed over only after personal-data wipe (items rule) |
| History & heritage | heritage | **Sub-category of knowledge** | A source per item; no divisive content |
| Culture & art | culture | **Sub-category of knowledge** (instruments → items, tickets → items, community art → time) | Free only |
| Spirituality & philosophy | spirituality | **Sub-category of knowledge** | No missionary content or solicitation; institutional religion excluded (D45) |

Domain hubs (S38) are not built; a sub-category has no hub of its own, only a filter inside its world's feed.

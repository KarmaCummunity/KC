# 08 — Organizations, verification, events, money

## Organizations
An org account is a separate entity managed by users in roles: org manager, volunteer manager, volunteer (S15); no 20-post limit. Each world hub has an org directory with search/filter/sort (D36), fed from the Registrar of Nonprofits open data (data.gov.il) with source attribution and a proper-management badge — we do **not** scrape GuideStar (S35). Any user may suggest a link; it goes live after admin approval (D9). Orgs affiliate volunteers (D22), publish events (D31), post jobs and recruit employees and volunteers (D41, D53), and approve hours (D32). **Every org registered in KC is verified** (D53). **For now everything is free for orgs**: KC gives them a tool parallel to the community's — posts, events, volunteers, hours, jobs (D55). A social venture is an org; a private person may register as an org (D57).

## Organization profile and accounts (D88–D92)
An organization has a **public profile** like a person's, adapted to organizations: logo and cover, name, type (nonprofit association, public-benefit company, public body, informal initiative, private venture per D57), what it does and where, website and social links, contact details, verification badges, its posts and followers; later its team (opt-in), activities, impact metrics and the transparency section from finance (D81). Behind the profile stands an **organization account**: the organization's own sign-in identity, plus members with roles who act in its name from their own accounts through a profile switcher (D88). Registration is a self-service wizard approved by a KC admin; any non-profit may register and the registration number is optional (D89). Profiles are always public and organizations may follow people and organizations (D90). Organizations publish in every world with no cap, members may post in the organization's name, and discovery runs through the Search "ארגונים" filter with sub-filters instead of a directory screen (D91). No custom layouts (D92). Catalog bodies imported from open data appear as **unclaimed** profiles the real organization can claim; the former external-bodies catalog and the registration applications fold into the one organization entity.

## Events
An event is a post type of an org, or of an affiliated volunteer on its behalf (D22, D31). Users register; the org chooses approval policy: manual, automatic, or rules such as "verified only". Org chooses the world; capacity and waitlist optional; org marks attendance — only attendance earns karma (S13). Closes at its date (D21). May recur: a weekly event is a series whose dates each take their own registrations (FR-POST-024). May kick off a group challenge (D43).
Tech: · not previously defined · time-world kind `org_event`: the post (`post_type = 'event'`) + `event_post_details` (organization, window, capacity, registration policy, address); only a member of the organization (any role) publishes it; an event is public, unlike the time world's followers-only default; registration is a `post_handoffs` row — `register_for_event`, `decide_event_registration`, `cancel_event_registration`, `list_event_registrations` (`0563`, G1 step 5a). App (G1 step 5b): the time world's offer form offers a member of an organization (`org_memberships`, any role) the choice between a volunteer shift and the organization's event, with an organization picker (preselected when there is one), start and end (end after start checked in the form, `event_window_invalid`), optional capacity, registration policy (automatic by default) and optional address, published through `create_marketplace_listing`; the post page shows the event section, a member's register (manual: request to join), pending / confirmed status and cancel, and the organizer's roster with approve / decline / remove (`IMarketplaceRepository` event methods, `@kc/domain` `eventPost`). The organizer's volunteer-opportunity create screen is retired; its button opens the one create form. Step 5c (`0564`): `post_handoffs.status` gains `waitlisted` — a full capacity event waitlists instead of refusing, and when a place frees (a registrant cancels, the organizer declines or removes one) the earliest waitlisted member moves up (confirmed, or waiting for approval under the manual policy), before the start only (`event_promote_waitlist`); a waitlisted member may leave. ## Org finance and transparency (D76–D82)
Every org — KC included, as the platform org — keeps its own books in a **finance sub-tree of its Admin Portal** (D76): single-entry ledger with financial accounts, a two-level category tree, calendar fiscal year, budgets and bank reconciliation (D78); entries come from manual input, CSV imports (bank, Bit, Paybox) and automatic mirroring of in-app payments (D79). New org-scoped roles: **org treasurer** (writes the books) and **org accountant** (reads and exports only); moderators keep read-only access to KC's books (D76). **Section 46(a)** is recorded, not decided: approval number, validity, certificate and expiry reminders per org, and a receipt record per donation (number, date, 46(a)-eligible flag, attached file); KC never generates official receipts, and KC itself holds no 46(a) approval today, so no tax-credit wording is shown until the status is active (D77). Smart tracking means computed reminders and queues plus counterparty-based auto-categorization, with a daily digest to treasurers (D80). Transparency is optional per org and published as frozen yearly snapshots — totals, program vs overhead share, public documents, 46(a) badge — never live books, and donor identities are never public (D81). The org profile that hosts the public transparency section is an extended user profile with its own email identity, posts and followers, plus the org Admin Portal (D82*, to be specified as its own domain).

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for your feedback on Guideline 5.1.1(v). This build lets anyone use the app's core content without creating an account.

How to browse without an account:
1. Launch the app. The first screen is the sign-in screen.
2. Tap "Browse without an account" (Hebrew interface: "צפייה ללא חשבון").
3. You can now read the community's open public posts (title, description and city) and load more with "Load more posts". Tap a post to open it.
4. No account, sign-in, email or other personal information is needed to browse.

An account is needed only for actions taken as a member: publishing a post, commenting, messaging someone or responding to an offer. When a guest wants to do one of these, the app offers "Sign in or create an account". Guests can go back to the sign-in screen at any time.

Registered members can delete their account in the app (Settings > Delete account).

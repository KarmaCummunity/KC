Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

1. Who we are

"Karma Community" (קהילת קארמה) operates the app and website. Data controller: Nave Sarussi. Contact: karmacommunity2.0@gmail.com.

2. Age

The service is for people aged 18 and over only. At sign-up you confirm that you are 18 or older, and we later ask for your date of birth; a date of birth under 18 is rejected. A parent who believes their child opened an account can contact us and the account will be removed.

3. Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, password (stored hashed by our sign-in provider) or the Google / Apple account you sign in with, profile photo, city, bio and date of birth.
  • Private details you choose to give: a contact phone number and an address (street and number). They are not shown on your profile; the phone number is shown only to a member you are in a direct chat with.
  • Content you create: posts, listings and photos, comments, chat messages, reactions, ratings, reports and blocks, event details (including the event address), saved ride routes and meeting points.
  • Emergency reports on a post: if you report an emergency, the note you write and the location you attach to the report are stored.
  • Surveys and feedback: your answers to in-app surveys and feedback you send.
  • Acceptance of the terms and this policy: when you accept the Terms of Use or this policy we store the version, time, language, IP address and browser/device identifier (user agent) as a record of your acceptance.
  • Organization applications: the organization details and contact person details (including a phone number) you submit when applying to add an organization.
  • Donations and subscriptions: if you donate money — amount, organization, status and transaction id. If an in-app paid subscription is offered, the purchase is made through Apple or Google and we receive the subscription status. We never store card details.
  • Technical: device type and operating system, app version, push token, the days you used the app, crash reports and usage events.
  • WhatsApp (if you use the bot): your phone number, the messages you send to the bot and the messages it sends you, and your consent status for receiving messages.
  • People who are not members: the community team keeps a contact list of partner organizations and their people (name, organization, email, phone, notes). Answers to public research questions are stored with a hash of the browser identifier, not the identifier itself.

4. Location

The app asks for your device's approximate location (accurate to about 100 meters), only while you use the app and only if you allow it:

  • Map — "near me": the location centers the map around you, and the app then loads the posts of the area shown.
  • Rides — "from my current location": the location picks the city nearest to you as the starting point.

This location is processed on the device and is not stored by us. Location is never used in the background. A location you choose yourself (city, street, saved route, meeting point, or the location in an emergency report) is stored as part of that content. A post's location is shown to others approximately, according to the setting you choose.

5. How we use it

  • To run the service: show posts and listings, match offers, requests and rides, chat and notifications.
  • To check age and eligibility.
  • Safety and moderation: handling reports and blocks, preventing spam and harassment, limiting the rate of actions.
  • Improving the service: usage statistics and crash reports.
  • To keep a record of your consents and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. Translation

The app's interface is shown in the language you choose, from language files built into the app. Automatic translation of member content is currently switched off, and your content is not sent to a translation service. If we turn such translation on, we will update this policy first.

  • Other members: your profile and public content are visible to the community according to your privacy settings.
  • The community team: authorized staff see the data needed to handle reports, applications and requests.
  • Service providers processing data for us:
    • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage.
    • Cloudflare — website hosting.
    • Expo — push notifications.
    • Google and Apple — sign-in, and in-app purchases.
    • RevenueCat — in-app subscription management (your user id and subscription status).
    • Meta / WhatsApp — the WhatsApp bot.
    • Sentry (US servers) — crash and performance reports.
    • PostHog (US servers) — in-app usage events; the events are not linked to your name or email.
    • OpenStreetMap — map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap's servers, so your device's IP address and the map area shown are visible to them.
    • A payment provider for donations. [TO CONFIRM: provider name]
  • Authorities: when required by law.

9. Retention

  • Account and content: while your account is active.
  • Account deletion: you can request deletion at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account). The request immediately hides your profile, posts, listings and chats from everyone else, and notifications stop. The account is deactivated and can be fully restored if you sign in again within 30 days. After 30 days it is permanently deleted: your profile, posts, comments, listings (including images and other files you uploaded), follows, blocks, reports, donation links and sign-in identity. Messages you sent stay with the people you talked to, without your name or photo.

  • Notifications: 180 days. Feed view log: 90 days. Days of use: 400 days.
  • WhatsApp: incoming messages — 90 days; outgoing messages and draft conversations with the bot — 180 days.
  • Audit log of moderation and admin actions: 24 months.

You may access, correct and delete your data, and get a copy of it in a machine-readable format. To get a copy of your data, contact karmacommunity2.0@gmail.com. You may also contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.
